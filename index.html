AI on Video Advertising:
What It Can Do, and What It Still Can't

"The future of AI is not about replacing humans, it's about augmenting human capabilities" — Sunder Pichai, CEO of Google

In video advertising, decisions are often made quickly and forgotten just as fast. Elements in ads—be it a colour choice, a headline tweak, length of a shot, or the pacing of an edit—are constantly tested, revised and replaced.

It is within this fast-paced advertising ecosystem that we must situate Artificial Intelligence, one of the most powerful and disruptive entrants the industry has encountered so far.

What AI Does Well in Video Advertising

Without a doubt, AI has improved advertisement and marketing industries in terms of speed, scale, and efficiency. One of the most visible contributions of AI in video advertising lies in automated production workflows.

Another area where AI adds tangible value is rapid content generation for low-stakes or functional video formats. Explainer videos, product demos, onboarding content, and internal communications are increasingly produced using AI-driven platforms.

Where AI Falls Short

While AI has proven effective in accelerating production and optimisation, its limitations become most visible when video advertising demands emotional coherence, cultural sensitivity, and narrative intent.

Video ads often rely on emotional cues: warmth, nostalgia, humour, tension, to create memorability and brand affinity. AI systems can replicate elements of human emotion, but they cannot natively understand why a joke hits right or why a specific image evokes nostalgia.
